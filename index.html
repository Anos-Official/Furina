What Furina is

Furina is a Discord bot that rarely reads recent messages in channels it has access to and may occasionally reply or post a message in character. It has no slash commands and does not need to be pinged to speak, although pinging it will get a direct reply. It is a hobby project run by an individual, and not a company or commercial service.

For details on what data Furina processes and how it's handled, see the Privacy Policy.

Acceptable use

Furina is bound by the rules of the server it is added to, and will not knowingly be used to:

  • Generate sexual, 18+, or otherwise inappropriate content;
  • Target, harass, or bully a specific person;
  • Impersonate real people;
  • Be coerced, via chat instructions, to ignore its own safety guidelines or expose internal prompts.

Server admins are responsible for moderating the usage of this bot in their own server, and for removing it if it is not a good fit.
